# Today's Plan

*Pick how long you have and get a short to-do list, best gold per minute first.*

Open the **Today** tab, or type `/ac plan`.

1. **Pick your time**, for example 15 minutes.
2. Read the list. Each step shows the gold it should bring in, after the AH cut.
3. Do each step and **tick it off**.

![Today's Plan for 15 minutes](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/ac-today.webp)
*Today's Plan for 15 minutes*

### What it can suggest

- **Post** items from your bags, bank and Warband Bank.
- **Repost** listings that were undercut.
- **Buy deals** you can afford, and items on your shopping list.
- **Vendor** things a vendor pays more for.
- **Collect your mailbox.**
- **Craft** things that clearly beat selling the materials.
- **Destroy** (disenchant, mill, prospect) when that beats selling.
- **Fast sellers:** items that sell quickly on your realm, bumped up the list.
