# Alerts and the website

*Windows and Discord alerts while you're away, and your report on the website.*

### Alerts

With the [desktop app](The-desktop-app.md), you get a Windows notification, and a Discord direct message if you link it, when:

- one of your listings is **undercut**,
- something on your **shopping list** hits your price,
- a listing **sold** while the game was closed,
- a listing **expired** without selling.

To link Discord, open the desktop app's settings from the tray coin and follow the steps: you post a code in the link channel on the CtrlShiftZed Discord and the bot does the rest.

> Sold and expired alerts are worked out from hourly Auction House snapshots, so a listing that ends in its last hour is reported once its time is up.

### The website

[auctioncoach.ctrlshiftzed.com](https://auctioncoach.ctrlshiftzed.com) has your report: your most valuable items, why listings aren't selling, where your gold went and a gold goal with a share link. The desktop app keeps it up to date, or paste `/ac export` under **My report**.
