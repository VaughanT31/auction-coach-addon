Make gold without learning TSM. Auction Coach tells you in plain English what your stuff is worth, what to sell, what price to post at and how likely it is to sell. No groups, operations or price formulas to set up. Open the Auction House once and the advice starts.

It advises and never automates. You do all the buying, posting and cancelling yourself.

  • Today's Plan: a to-do list for the time you have, best gold per minute first.
  • What it's worth: the value of everything on all your characters, right in the tooltip.
  • What to sell: one suggested price for everything in your bags, and the chance it sells.
  • Deals and shopping: items listed below their usual price, flips between your realms and a shopping list.
  • Your auctions and gold: what to do about each listing, every sale reported, and your gold over time.

Today's Plan
Got 15 minutes? Pick 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes and get a short to-do list, best gold per minute first. Collect your mailbox, post what's worth posting from your bags, bank and warband bank, repost undercut listings, cancel what a vendor now pays more for, buy the deals you can afford and vendor the rest. When something on your shopping list is cheap it goes near the top, and when disenchanting, milling or prospecting beats selling, the plan says so. Each step shows the gold it should actually bring in after the AH cut, not the best case. Tick steps off as you go.

What Your Stuff Is Worth
Hover any item to see what it's worth, how many you have across your characters, whether a vendor pays more and how tough the competition is. The tooltip also says what it's worth disenchanted, milled or prospected and whether that beats selling it. The My Stuff tab adds up everything sellable on all your characters and your warband bank, item by item. Most players are surprised by how much is sitting in their banks.

What to Sell, and for How Much
The Sell tab lists everything in your bags with one suggested price, how fast it sells and whether to post, hold or vendor it. Put an item in the sell box and the post helper beside the Auction House suggests a price and shows the chance your stack sells in time. "Use this price" fills it in, and you still click Post.

Your Auctions
The Auctions tab shows every listing on this character, your price next to the lowest one, and what to do about each: keep it, repost it at a new price, cancel it because a vendor now pays more, or let it run because reposting isn't worth it today. Auction Coach learns how fast each item gets undercut, so you know which ones need watching, and a chat line tells you what each sale made after the AH cut.

Deals, Shopping and Flips
The Deals tab lists items listed well below their usual price that sell every day, with your profit after the AH cut. Click a row at the AH to open its listings. Add items to your shopping list with the most you'll pay, and Auction Coach tells you in the list, the tooltip and chat when one is listed at or below that. Other realms shows items that are cheap on one of your realms and usually sell for more on another: buy, move it through the warband bank, post.

Destroy Values
There's no game data for what items destroy into, so Auction Coach learns it from what you get each time you disenchant, mill or prospect, including herbs and ore straight from your bank. With the desktop app, it also uses the average from other players who share theirs, so values show from the start. /ac destroy lists everything learned.

Your Gold
The gold overview shows each character's gold and the warband bank's. History shows how your gold changed today, this week and this month, with your AH sales and purchases day by day. Hover a day for each character's gold that day.

Safety and Fit

  • Vendor and delete protection: warns before you sell or delete something valuable, and offers to buy it straight back from the vendor.
  • Grey items: says to vendor them. Most grey AH listings are gold sellers moving gold, so their prices aren't real.
  • Fits how you play: tell it whether you check the AH rarely, often or constantly, and the advice adapts.

Desktop App and Website (Optional)
The addon works on its own from its own AH scans. The free Windows app sits in the tray and downloads fresh prices every hour, with 14-day averages, sales estimates and shared destroy values, which make deals, sell chances and Today's Plan much better. Windows may warn about the unsigned installer: click More info, then Run anyway. On the website, search any item's price, or paste /ac export to see what all your characters' items are worth.

Commands

  • /ac: open or close the window (or click the minimap button, right-click for options)
  • /ac plan: Today's Plan
  • /ac auctions: your auctions and what to do about each
  • /ac gold: your gold and AH sales over time
  • /ac destroy: what's been learned about disenchanting, milling and prospecting
  • /ac shop add [item] 12.5: add an item to your shopping list with the most you'll pay
  • /ac help: every command

Good to Know

  • EU and US regions.
  • Sell chances and sales per day are estimates, and get better with the desktop app's data.
  • Blizzard allows one full AH scan every 15 minutes, shared by all addons. If another addon just scanned, Auction Coach waits. /ac status shows what the scanner is doing.
  • Bugs and ideas: leave a comment or open an issue on GitHub.
