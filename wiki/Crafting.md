# Crafting

*Which recipes make money, the cheapest materials for each quality, and destroy values.*

### Craft or sell

The **Craft** view on the Deals tab lists every recipe your characters know, with what its materials cost against what the crafted item sells for, at the quality you actually make. Enchants count as the scroll they make on Enchanting Vellum, which is what sells (the vellum isn't in the cost).

![Material cost against what each craft sells for](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/ac-craft.webp)
*Material cost against what each craft sells for*

### Cheapest materials

Beside the profession window, Auction Coach shows each quality you can make with the cheapest mix of material qualities that gets there, its cost and profit. **Use** fills the recipe in. The **+** on a row adds that craft's materials to your shopping list, less what you already have.

Season sparks aren't counted as a crafting cost.

### Crafting orders

Commission from crafting orders you fill for other players is counted as crafting-order income in [Accounting](Accounting.md).

### Destroying

Auction Coach learns what you get from disenchanting, milling and prospecting. Tooltips then say what an item is worth destroyed, and Today's Plan tells you when that beats selling. `/ac destroy` shows what it has learned. With the desktop app, everyone's results are shared, so values show before you've destroyed anything yourself.
