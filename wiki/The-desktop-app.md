# The desktop app

*Fresh prices every hour, sales estimates and alerts while the game is closed.*

The desktop app is free and optional. It sits in the Windows tray and downloads fresh prices every hour, with 14-day averages and sales estimates.

### Install it

1. **Download** the [desktop app installer](https://auctioncoach.ctrlshiftzed.com/v1/desktop/download).
2. **Run it.** Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't code signed yet: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. No admin rights needed.
3. **Pick your WoW folder.** It usually finds it: click it, then **Continue**.
4. **Pick your realms.** Your characters' realms are ticked if you've logged in with the addon. Check the **Region** (EU or US), tick any others you play, then **Save realms**.
5. **Wait for "Prices are up to date"**, then click **Finish**.
6. **In game, type `/reload`.** The Auction Coach window now says "Prices updated ... ago" at the top right.

From then on it starts with Windows. The game loads new prices when you log in or `/reload`.

### Change or remove it

- **Settings:** double-click the Auction Coach coin in the Windows tray.
- **Remove:** Windows **Settings > Apps > Auction Coach**.

### No desktop app?

Type `/ac export` in game, paste it under **My report** on [the website](https://auctioncoach.ctrlshiftzed.com/#/report), then copy the prices string it gives you back into `/ac import`.

The app also sends alerts while the game is closed. See [Alerts and the website](Alerts-and-the-website.md).
