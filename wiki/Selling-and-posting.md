# Selling and posting

*The Sell tab, the post helper, your auctions and why something isn't selling.*

### The Sell tab

Lists everything in your bags with one suggested price, how fast it sells and whether to **post**, **hold** or **vendor** it. At the Auction House, click a row to put the item in the sell box.

![The Sell tab](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/ac-sell.webp)
*The Sell tab*

### The post helper

Put an item in the Auction House sell box and the post helper suggests a price and shows the chance your stack sells in time. **Use this price** fills it in. You still click Post.

![The post helper beside the sell box](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/ac-post-helper.webp)
*The post helper beside the sell box*

### Your auctions

The **Auctions** tab (`/ac auctions`) shows which of your listings are undercut and what to do about each. Partly sold stacks show, for example, *112 sold*.

### Why isn't it selling?

Hover a listing on the Auctions tab for a plain answer: someone listed it cheaper, your price is over what it usually sells for, it simply sells slowly on your realm, or it's just early days. It also says how many cheaper listings are ahead of yours and about how long to expect.

### Sale messages

When an auction sells, a chat line says so. The game doesn't say how many of a stack sold, so the mailbox gives the exact amount when you open it. Turn the messages off or on with `/ac sales`.
