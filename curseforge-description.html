Make gold without learning TSM. Auction Coach tells you in plain English what your stuff is worth, what to sell, what price to post at and how likely it is to sell. No groups, operations or price formulas to set up. Open the Auction House once and the advice starts.

It advises and never automates. You do all the buying, posting and cancelling yourself.

  • Today's Plan: a to-do list for the time you have, best gold per minute first.
  • What it's worth: the value of everything on all your characters, right in the tooltip.
  • What to sell: one suggested price for everything in your bags, and the chance it sells.
  • Deals and shopping: items listed below their usual price, flips between your realms and a shopping list.
  • Your auctions and gold: what to do about each listing, cheapest or undercut right on the Auction House, every sale reported, and your gold over time.
  • Crafting: what every recipe costs to craft and the profit, under the recipe itself.
  • Mailbox and reposting: one-click mail collecting, the gold still on its way, and a repost list that posts your regulars one click each.
  • Accounting: where your gold comes from and goes, and why a listing isn't selling.

Version 1.0 is here: the full release, with the mailbox, repost list, sell lists, accounting and more. See the changelog for everything new.

Today's Plan
Got 15 minutes? Pick 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes and get a short to-do list, best gold per minute first. Collect your mailbox, post what's worth posting from your bags, bank and warband bank, repost undercut listings, cancel what a vendor now pays more for, buy the deals you can afford and vendor the rest. When something on your shopping list is cheap it goes near the top, and when disenchanting, milling or prospecting beats selling, the plan says so. Each step shows the gold it should actually bring in after the AH cut, not the best case. Fast sellers are marked and come first in a near tie, and under the plan, Sells fast here lists what trades the most gold a day on your realm, worth farming or crafting. Tick steps off as you go.

What Your Stuff Is Worth
Hover any item to see what it's worth, how many you have across your characters, whether a vendor pays more and how tough the competition is. The tooltip also says what it's worth disenchanted, milled or prospected and whether that beats selling it. The My Stuff tab adds up everything sellable on all your characters and your warband bank, item by item. Most players are surprised by how much is sitting in their banks.

What to Sell, and for How Much
The Sell tab lists everything in your bags with one suggested price, how fast it sells and whether to post, hold or vendor it. Put an item in the sell box and the post helper beside the Auction House suggests a price and shows the chance your stack sells in time. "Use this price" fills it in, and you still click Post.

New: Cheapest or Undercut, Right on the Auction House
Blizzard's own Auctions tab gets a Status column after Time left. Each of your auctions says Cheapest, or Undercut with how much lower the cheapest listing is and, in brackets, how many cheaper auctions other sellers have (units, for reagents and other stackables). Opening the tab or pressing refresh checks each item's current price for you, one at a time, and the column updates as the answers come in. Hover it for the prices. Turn it off in the options if you'd rather not have it.

New: Is It Worth Crafting?
Under every recipe you know, cooking and feasts included, the profession window shows To craft (what the materials you've chosen in the recipe cost) and Profit (what it makes at the quality those materials reach, the same as Blizzard's Crafting Details, after the AH cut). Hover it for the materials one by one. Add to shopping list asks how many you want to make and puts the materials on your shopping list, less what you already have. Auction Coach also remembers what vendors charge whenever you open one, so recipes with butter, spirits, flux and other vendor materials are costed too, and the shopping list says to buy those from a vendor.

New: A Gold Goal You Can Share
Set a gold goal and a deadline on the Auction Coach website's Gold goal page and see each day how far you've come, the gold a day it needs, your own pace and when you'll get there, with milestones along the way. Paste your /ac export to fill it, or turn on Gold goal on the website in the free desktop app and it keeps itself up to date. Share the link: it shows a name you pick, and gold amounts only if you allow it. Off unless you use it, and it only ever holds one number a day, your account's total gold.

New: Accounting
See where your gold comes from and where it goes. Every change in your gold is sorted by what caused it, from AH sales and vendor trips to repairs, flight paths, quests and looted gold. View today, this week, this month or all time, for one character, a realm or your whole account, with your net gold and your biggest cost on top. Gold moved between your own characters is kept out of the totals, and your website report shows the last 7 and 30 days.

New: Why Isn't It Selling?
Hover any of your listings for a plain answer: someone is cheaper, you're over the usual price, it's a slow seller on your realm, there's a queue of cheaper listings ahead of you and how long it takes to sell through, or it's a fast seller and you only need to wait. Your website report checks every listing against today's Auction House and lists the fastest sellers on your realms.

New: Sell Lists for Your Other Realms
Tick your items that sell for more on another of your realms and they go on that realm's sell list. At the bank, one click moves them into the warband bank, and on the other realm one click takes them out, ready for the repost list to post at its Auction House.

New: A Repost List
Keep the items you post again and again on a list beside the Auction House. Post next posts each one at Auction Coach's price with a click, never under the floor you set, checking the price first when it's old. It shows what's ready, what's under its floor and what's worth holding, and items go on the list straight from the post helper or by dragging them onto it. Keep items in groups you switch on and off, and add batches to sell once: what came back from expired or cancelled auctions, or a checklist of your bags. Each comes off the list by itself once it's sold.

New: Mailbox Buttons and Gold on the Way
The mailbox opens on Auction Coach's own inbox: every mail in one searchable list with its type, days left and gold, a button that collects all Auction House mail, and one each for sold, bought, cancelled, expired, outbid and other mail. Mail from other players opens as normal, anything with cash on delivery is never collected, and one button swaps back to Blizzard's inbox. The gold from a sale arrives by mail an hour later, so My Stuff shows the gold on the way, when the next mail lands and what's waiting in your mailbox, per character.

Your Auctions and Exact Sales
The Auctions tab shows every listing on this character, your price next to the lowest one, and what to do about each: keep it, repost it at a new price, cancel it because a vendor now pays more, or let it run because reposting isn't worth it today. Auction Coach learns how fast each item gets undercut, so you know which ones need watching, and when something sells you hear about it: the game doesn't say how many of a stack went, so the mailbox gives the exact amount and gold after the AH cut, and a partly sold stack shows how many sold. With the desktop app, sales while you're offline arrive as a Discord message or Windows notification, with the exact amount.

Deals, Shopping and Flips
Needs the desktop app's prices (see below). The Deals tab lists items listed well below where they resell that sell at least 10 a day and aren't buried in other listings, with your profit after the AH cut. The resell price never goes above the next listings up, so a few cheap items under a big stack show what you'd really make. Click a row at the AH to open its listings. Alt-click any item (or pick another click in the options, or set a key in Keybindings and press it over any item) to put it on your shopping list with the most you'll pay, and Auction Coach tells you in the list, the tooltip and chat when one is listed at or below that. At the Auction House the list opens beside it, grouped by where each item came from, and the + on a Cheapest materials row adds a whole craft's materials. Other realms shows items that are cheap on one of your realms and usually sell for more on another, grouped by route (buy on this realm, sell on that one): buy, move it through the warband bank, post. Items you already have are compared too: their tooltip says when another of your realms pays clearly more (hold Shift to see why it doesn't), and Other realms lists them at the top, ready to move through the warband bank. Set how many a day a flip must sell in the options, and tick flips to add them to your shopping list at the most you can pay and still make your profit.

Gold Per Hour
Click Gold per hour in the Auction Coach window (or Shift-click the minimap button, or type /ac gph) and press Start when you head out farming. Auction Coach counts the gold you loot and what your looted and gathered items are worth on the AH (or at the vendor when that pays more), and shows your gold per hour, your best items and a history of past sessions with where you farmed. You start, pause and stop it yourself, so keys and raids never count, and a /reload doesn't lose it. The minimise button beside the X shrinks it to just the numbers while you farm.

Cheapest Materials
Open one of your recipes and a panel beside the profession window lists every quality you can make, with the cheapest mix of material qualities that gets there, what those materials sell for, and the profit when each quality is its own item. Concentration rows show up when concentration reaches a quality with cheaper materials, with how much it costs. Hover a row for the exact materials, and click Use to put them in the recipe. The game itself says which quality each mix makes, so it follows Blizzard's rules exactly.

Craft or Sell
The Deals tab's Craft view lists your characters' recipes with what one craft's materials sell for against what the crafted item sells for, after the AH cut. Crafts that make money come first; for the rest, selling the materials makes more. Open each character's profession window once and Auction Coach learns their recipes, and which quality each character makes, so that quality is the one priced. If concentration would make the next quality, it says how much more that's worth. Hovering a crafted item says what crafting it makes, and Today's Plan adds a craft step when a recipe clearly beats selling the materials you have.

Manipulation Warnings
When someone buys out the cheap listings and relists them far higher, the tooltip and post helper say the price looks pushed up, so you don't buy into it or count on that price. Deals whose usual price looks pushed up are marked and kept out of Today's Plan.

Market Calendar Hints
Today's Plan notes when today usually means higher or lower prices for consumables and trade goods, and what the Darkmoon Faire usually does in its week. The post helper says which day they usually sell best. From the server's price history, through the desktop app.

Destroy Values
There's no game data for what items destroy into, so Auction Coach learns it from what you get each time you disenchant, mill or prospect, including herbs and ore straight from your bank. With the desktop app, it also uses the average from other players who share theirs, so values show from the start. /ac destroy lists everything learned.

Your Gold
The gold overview shows each character's gold and the warband bank's. History shows how your gold changed today, this week and this month, with your AH sales and purchases day by day. Hover a day for each character's gold that day.

Safety and Fit

  • Vendor and delete protection: warns before you sell or delete something valuable, and offers to buy it straight back from the vendor.
  • Grey items: says to vendor them. Most grey AH listings are gold sellers moving gold, so their prices aren't real.
  • Fits how you play: tell it whether you check the AH rarely, often or constantly, and the advice adapts.

Desktop App and Website (Optional)
The addon works on its own from its own AH scans. The free Windows app sits in the tray and downloads fresh prices every hour, with 14-day averages, sales estimates and shared destroy values, which make sell chances and Today's Plan much better. Deals and Other realms need the app: your own scans can't tell an item's usual price or how many sell a day. It can also alert you by Windows notification or Discord message: undercuts, shopping list prices, expired listings, and exactly what sold while you were offline. It tells you when a new version is out. Windows may warn about the unsigned installer: click More info, then Run anyway. On the website, search any item's price, or paste /ac export to see what all your characters' items are worth.

Commands

  • /ac: open or close the window (or click the minimap button, right-click for options)
  • /ac plan: Today's Plan
  • /ac auctions: your auctions and what to do about each
  • /ac gold: your gold and AH sales over time
  • /ac destroy: what's been learned about disenchanting, milling and prospecting
  • /ac shop add [item] 12.5: add an item to your shopping list with the most you'll pay
  • /ac help: every command

Good to Know

  • EU and US regions.
  • Sell chances and sales per day are estimates, and get better with the desktop app's data.
  • Blizzard allows one full AH scan every 15 minutes, shared by all addons. If another addon just scanned, Auction Coach waits. /ac status shows what the scanner is doing.
  • Bugs and ideas: leave a comment or open an issue on GitHub.
