Auction Coach is a plain-English Auction House coach: what your stuff is worth, what to sell, at what price and how likely it is to sell. No groups or formulas. It advises and never automates.

Today's Plan
Got 15 minutes? Pick your time and get a short to-do list, best gold per minute first: what to post, repost, buy and vendor, with the gold each step makes.

What It's Worth
Tooltips say what an item is worth, how many you have on all characters, if a vendor pays more and how tough the competition is. After a few destroys, they add the disenchant, mill or prospect value. My Stuff adds up everything sellable, with your gold and its history.

Sell and Post
The Sell tab prices everything in your bags: post, hold or vendor. In the sell box, the post helper suggests a price and the chance it sells in time.

Deals
Items well below their usual price, with your profit. Plus a shopping list that tells you when an item hits your price, and flips between your realms.

Undercuts, Sales and Safety
Learns undercut speed, adapts to how often you check the AH, reports each sale and warns before you vendor or delete something valuable.

Desktop App and Website
Works alone. The free Windows app adds hourly prices, 14-day averages and sales estimates. On the website, search any item or paste /ac export to value all your characters.

Getting Started

  • Open the AH once to start.
  • /ac or the minimap button opens the window, /ac help lists commands.

Good to Know
Data covers EU and US. Sales are estimates. Windows may warn about the unsigned installer: More info, then Run anyway.
