# Mailbox and reposting

*A searchable inbox, the repost list and sell lists for your other realms.*

### The inbox

At the mailbox, every mail is in one searchable list, with one click to collect all Auction House mail or just one kind. Each sale shows how many sold and the gold after the AH cut.

![The Auction Coach inbox](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/ac-mailbox.webp)
*The Auction Coach inbox*

### The repost list

Items you post again and again, beside the Auction House (`/ac repost`). Each one posts with one click at Auction Coach's price, never under the floor you set.

- **Add items** from your bags, from items that expired or were cancelled, or from a group.
- **Groups:** sets you switch on and off together.
- **Sell once:** a batch that leaves the list by itself once it's sold.

![The repost list beside the Auction House](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/ac-repost.webp)
*The repost list beside the Auction House*

### Sell lists

Plan a sale on one realm, move the items through the Warband Bank, and post them on another.

1. Open **Deals**, then **Other realms**. Tick your items that sell for more on another of your realms, then click **Add yours to sell lists**.
2. Open your bank on the character holding them. The **Sell lists** panel opens beside the bank window, with a **Put in** button for each item in your bags. Items still in your character bank get a **To bags** button first.
3. Log in on a character on the realm you're selling on and open the bank there. The panel lists the items in the Warband Bank with a **Take out** button.
4. Go to the Auction House. They're on the repost list, one click each to post, and each one leaves the sell list once it's posted.

> The panel only opens at a bank, and only when you have a sell list. It works with ElvUI and other bag addons. To turn it off, untick **Sell lists at the bank** in the options.
