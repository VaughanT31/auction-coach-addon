# Crafting

*Which recipes make money, lumber crafting, the cheapest materials for each quality, and destroy values.*

### Craft or sell

The **Craft** view on the Deals tab lists every recipe your characters know, with what its materials cost against what the crafted item sells for, at the quality you actually make. Enchants count as the scroll they make on Enchanting Vellum, which is what sells (the vellum isn't in the cost).

*Material cost against what each craft sells for*

### Multicraft, resourcefulness and ingenuity

Profit includes what multicraft and resourcefulness add on average, using your character's own chances on that recipe. Hover a row or the profit under a recipe to see how much each adds. The concentration line counts ingenuity's refund too. Your stats are read when you open that profession's window on that character, and what a proc is worth is an average, not a promise.

### Cheapest materials

Beside the profession window, Auction Coach shows each quality you can make with the cheapest mix of material qualities that gets there, its cost and profit. **Use** fills the recipe in. The **+** on a row adds that craft's materials to your shopping list, less what you already have.

The **Cheapest materials** button beside the profession window's tabs turns the panel on and off. The panel stacks with the other CtrlShiftZed side panels (Patron Planner's order queue, Auction Coach's Cheapest materials, Alt Inventory's Other characters) instead of covering them, the order queue on top. A button for each sits beside the window's tabs: a gold line means it's on.

Season sparks aren't counted as a crafting cost.

### Lumber Crafting

The **Lumber** tab (`/ac lumber`) lists every profession recipe that uses lumber, from every expansion and every profession, including ones none of your characters know.

Lumber is Warbound, so it never sells on the Auction House and isn't counted as a cost. But you only have so much, so each recipe shows the **gold each lumber makes**: the decor's lowest listing right now after the AH cut, less the other materials at their cheapest quality, divided by the lumber it takes. The list is sorted by how many of the decor sell a day, most first.

**Lowest now** is the cheapest listing: from the desktop app's hourly prices, or fresher when you search the item at the Auction House. It turns amber when someone is selling well under the usual price; hover for the usual lowest.

- **Worth it:** makes gold and sells at least once a day.
- **Slow seller:** makes gold, but sells less than once a day.
- **Not worth it:** at the lowest listing, the other materials cost more than the decor makes. Craft it for your own house instead.
- **Not on AH** or **No price:** nobody is selling the decor, or a material has no price yet.

**A day** is how many of that decor sell a day, in amber when it's less than one. The **Recipe** column says whether one of your characters knows it, can learn it (has the profession), or no one can. Hover a row for the materials, how much of that lumber you have across your characters and the warband bank, and where the recipe is learned: the trainer, or the recipe item that teaches it with its AH price. Open each crafter's profession window once so Auction Coach knows their recipes and the game's own "where to learn it" text. The warband bank's lumber is counted when you open a bank.

Use **Show** to pick Known, Known + can learn or All, and **Lumber** for one lumber type.

### Crafting orders

Commission from crafting orders you fill for other players is counted as crafting-order income in [Accounting](Accounting.md).

### Destroying

Auction Coach learns what you get from disenchanting, milling and prospecting. Tooltips then say what an item is worth destroyed, and Today's Plan tells you when that beats selling. `/ac destroy` shows what it has learned. With the desktop app, everyone's results are shared, so values show before you've destroyed anything yourself.
