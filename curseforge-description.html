Auction Coach is a plain-English Auction House coach: what your stuff is worth, what to sell, at what price and how likely it is to sell. No groups or formulas. It advises and never automates.

Today's Plan
Got 15 minutes? Pick your time and get a short to-do list, best gold per minute first: what to post, repost, buy and vendor, with the gold each step should make.

What It's Worth
Tooltips say what an item is worth, how many you have on all characters, if a vendor pays more and how tough the competition is. My Stuff adds up everything sellable, warband bank included.

Sell and Post
The Sell tab prices everything in your bags: post, hold or vendor. In the sell box, the post helper suggests a price and the chance it sells in time.

Deals
Items listed well below their usual lowest price, with your profit after the AH cut. One click opens it.

Undercuts, Sales and Safety
Learns how fast items get undercut, adapts to how often you check the AH, reports what each sale made and warns before you vendor or delete something valuable.

Desktop App and Website
Works alone from its own scans. The free Windows app adds hourly prices, 14-day averages and sales estimates. On the website, search any item or paste /ac export to value all your characters.

Getting Started

  • Open the AH once and the advice starts.
  • /ac or the minimap button opens the window, /ac plan opens Today's Plan, /ac help lists commands.

Good to Know
Data covers EU and US. Sales are estimates. Windows may warn about the unsigned installer: More info, then Run anyway.
