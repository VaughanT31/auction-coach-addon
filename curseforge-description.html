Auction Coach is a plain-English Auction House coach: what your stuff is worth, what to sell, what price to post at and how likely it is to sell. No groups or formulas. It advises and never automates.

What It's Worth
Tooltips say what an item is worth, how many you have across your characters, if a vendor pays more and how hard the competition is. My Stuff adds up everything sellable on all your characters and your warband bank.

Sell and Post
The Sell tab suggests a price for everything in your bags and whether to post, hold or vendor it. Put an item in the sell box and the post helper suggests a price and shows the chance it sells in time.

Deals
Items listed well below their usual lowest price, with your profit after the AH cut. One click opens it.

Undercuts, Sales and Safety
Learns how fast each item gets undercut, adapts to how often you check the AH (casual, active or camper), tells you what each sale made and warns before you vendor or delete something valuable.

Desktop App and Website
Works alone from its own scans. The free Windows app adds hourly prices, 14-day averages and sales estimates, which power deals and sell chances. On the website, search any item or paste /ac export to see what all your characters are worth.

Getting Started

  • Open the AH once: Auction Coach scans it and the advice starts.
  • /ac or the minimap button opens the window, /ac options sets your seller style.
  • /ac help lists every command.

Good to Know
App data covers EU and US: commodities everywhere, gear on realms in use. Sales are estimates. Windows may warn about the unsigned installer: choose More info, then Run anyway.
