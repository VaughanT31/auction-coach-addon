# Auction Coach wiki

Everything you need to use Auction Coach: setting it up, the desktop app, Today's Plan, selling, deals, crafting, the mailbox, accounting, alerts and the website report.

Also on the website: [Auction Coach wiki](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/wiki/auction-coach/) | Get it on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auction-coach)

## Pages

- [Getting started](Getting-started.md): Install the addon, open the Auction House once, and the advice starts.
- [The desktop app](The-desktop-app.md): Fresh prices every hour, sales estimates and alerts while the game is closed.
- [Today's Plan](Today-s-Plan.md): Pick how long you have and get a short to-do list, best gold per minute first.
- [What your stuff is worth](What-your-stuff-is-worth.md): My Stuff, item tooltips, your gold overview and gold history.
- [Selling and posting](Selling-and-posting.md): The Sell tab, the post helper, your auctions and why something isn't selling.
- [Deals and shopping](Deals-and-shopping.md): Cheap listings worth buying, your shopping list and flips between your realms.
- [Crafting](Crafting.md): Which recipes make money, the cheapest materials for each quality, and destroy values.
- [Mailbox and reposting](Mailbox-and-reposting.md): A searchable inbox, the repost list and sell lists for your other realms.
- [Accounting](Accounting.md): Where your gold comes from and where it goes.
- [Alerts and the website](Alerts-and-the-website.md): Windows and Discord alerts while you're away, and your report on the website.
- [Commands](Commands.md): Every Auction Coach chat command.
- [Questions and fixes](Questions-and-fixes.md): Answers to the things people ask most.
