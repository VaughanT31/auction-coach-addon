# Deals and shopping

*Cheap listings worth buying, your shopping list and flips between your realms.*

### Deals

The **Deals** tab lists items listed well below their usual price that sell at least 10 a day and aren't buried in other listings, with your profit after the AH cut. At the Auction House, click a row to open its listings. Buying is up to you.

### Shopping list

- **Alt-click** any item (bags, chat, the Auction House) to add it, with the most you'll pay.
- Auction Coach tells you, in the list, the tooltip and chat, when one is listed at or below that.
- **Buying several of an item:** reagents and other commodities let you type how many. Gear, pets and other items are one auction per listing, and Blizzard needs a Buyout click for each, so Auction Coach selects the cheapest listing at or under your max for you, and the next one after each Buyout. Just keep clicking **Buyout**. Turn it off with **Select the next listing to buy** in the options.
- Right-click an item in the list to edit or remove it.
- At the Auction House the list opens beside it, grouped by who added each item (you, Patron Planner, a craft).
- From chat: `/ac shop add [item] 12.5`, `/ac shop remove [item]`, `/ac shop alerts`.

![The shopping list beside the Auction House](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/ac-shopping.webp)
*The shopping list beside the Auction House*

### Other realms

Items that are cheap on one of your realms and usually sell for more on another. Buy, move it through the Warband Bank, post.

- **Your items come first:** things you already have that sell for clearly more on another of your realms. Tick them and click **Add yours to sell lists**.
- **Filters above the list:** pick an Auction House category (Weapons, Armor, Battle Pets...), the smallest profit and how many a day it must sell there. Each category shows its own best 100.
- **Reagents never show:** commodities share one Auction House across every realm, so they cost the same everywhere.
- **Tooltips say why not:** on an item you have, the tooltip says why another realm isn't better, for example it pays less than 25% more or sells too slowly there. Hold Shift to see it on any item.
- **Where it pays the most:** the tooltip also says which of your realms pays the most for it, whatever the margin. Handy when you're holding stock for a sale day.
