# Deals and shopping

*Cheap listings worth buying, your shopping list and flips between your realms.*

### Deals

The **Deals** tab lists items listed well below their usual price that sell at least 10 a day and aren't buried in other listings, with your profit after the AH cut. At the Auction House, click a row to open its listings. Buying is up to you.

![Deals: items listed well below their usual price](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/ac-deals.webp)
*Deals: items listed well below their usual price*

### Shopping list

- **Alt-click** any item (bags, chat, the Auction House) to add it, with the most you'll pay.
- Auction Coach tells you, in the list, the tooltip and chat, when one is listed at or below that.
- Right-click an item in the list to edit or remove it.
- At the Auction House the list opens beside it, grouped by who added each item (you, Patron Planner, a craft).
- From chat: `/ac shop add [item] 12.5`, `/ac shop remove [item]`, `/ac shop alerts`.

![The shopping list beside the Auction House](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/ac-shopping.webp)
*The shopping list beside the Auction House*

### Other realms

Items that are cheap on one of your realms and usually sell for more on another. Buy, move it through the Warband Bank, post.

![Flips between your realms](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/ac-flips.webp)
