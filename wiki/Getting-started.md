# Getting started

*Install the addon, open the Auction House once, and the advice starts.*

Auction Coach tells you, in plain English, what your stuff is worth, what to sell, what price to post at and how likely it is to sell. There are no groups, operations or price formulas to set up.

**It advises and never automates.** Every buy, post and cancel is your click.

### Install

1. **CurseForge app:** search for **Auction Coach** and click Install.
2. **By hand:** download it from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auction-coach) and put the **AuctionCoach** folders in `World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns`.
3. Start the game, or type `/reload`.

### First steps

1. **Open the Auction House once.** Auction Coach scans it, and the advice starts straight away.
2. Type `/ac` or click the minimap button to open the window.
3. Start with **Today**, the plan tab. See [Today's Plan](Today-s-Plan.md).
4. Open your bank and Warband Bank once on each character, so **My Stuff** can count what you own.

### Make it better (optional)

The free desktop app downloads fresh prices every hour, with 14-day averages and sales estimates. Deals, sell chances and Today's Plan get much better with it. See [The desktop app](The-desktop-app.md).

### Tell it how you play

In the options (`/ac options`), pick your **seller style**:

| Style | You... |
|---|---|
| **Casual** | Check the AH a few times a day |
| **Active** | Check about every hour |
| **Camper** | Sit at the AH and repost |

The advice adapts: a casual seller is pointed at prices that hold for hours, a camper at quicker reposts.
