# Accounting

*Where your gold comes from and where it goes.*

Open the **Accounting** tab, or type `/ac accounting`.

It shows your income and spending by category, per character, per realm or for your whole account:

- **Auction House:** sales, purchases and deposits.
- **Vendors:** sales and purchases, and repairs.
- **Crafting orders**, **trades**, **mail** and the **guild bank**.
- **Looted gold** and **quests**.
- **Flight paths**, **trainers**, **transmog** and the **barber**.

![Accounting: income and spending by category](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/ac-accounting.webp)
*Accounting: income and spending by category*

Gold moved between your own characters (Warband Bank, mail to your alts) is kept apart, so it doesn't count as income or spending.

> Accounting counts from the day you installed 1.0. Older gold history is in the gold overview's History.
